# Employee Promotion & Temporary Access Assignment Lab

## Objective 
Simulate and employee promotion requiring temporary cross department access while maintaining role based access control and documenting the business justification.

## Why This Lab Matters
Promotions often require additional permissions beyond a users primary role. Administrators must balance operational needs with security by granting only the necessary access, documenting the reason, and planning for future access reviews.

## Steps Performed
- Updated Michael Davis job title
- Retained IT department membership
- Added temporary Finance Security Group membership
- Verified Active Directory attributes and group memberships
- Doccumented the business justification through a simulated service desk ticket
